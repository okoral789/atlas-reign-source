# Original 2D equipment silhouettes

`tools/frontier/generate_nuke_markers.py` draws six original full-color transparent
256×256 PNGs: Atom, Hydrogen, MIRV, Siegebreaker (`mega`), Comet Cluster (`scatter`)
and warhead. PNGs are dedicated under CC0-1.0. The generator remains under the
project source licence. `generated.json` records hashes; `uploaded.json` records
owner-hosted Roblox uploads. No third-party art or 3D meshes are used.

The same images are used in Armory and on the map. Equipment colors stay fixed;
a small stern band and the flight trail carry the owner color. Map missiles turn
along their actual flight arc. Existing atlas art is retained during loading.
`preview.png` is an asset review at multiple sizes/angles, not a client screenshot.
